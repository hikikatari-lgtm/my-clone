Introduction

Background information about the story

Volcanoes are some of the most powerful and dangerous things in nature. When a volcano erupts, it can destroy everything around it.

This is the story of Harry Dalton, a volcanologist who works for the United States Geological Survey. He travels to a small town called Dante's Peak in the mountains of Washington State. The town is beautiful and peaceful, but there is a big mountain above it — and the mountain is a volcano.

Before You Read

  1. What do you know about volcanoes? Think about famous eruptions you have heard about.
  2. Would you like to live near a volcano? Why or why not? There is no right answer — think about the risks and the beauty.

Chapter 1 — A New Beginning

Harry Dalton arrives in Dante's Peak

Harry Dalton drove his truck into the small town of Dante's Peak. It was a beautiful place. The town was in a valley between tall mountains, and there was a big lake nearby. The people in the town were friendly and happy.

Harry was a volcanologist. His job was to study volcanoes and tell people when a volcano was going to erupt. Four years ago, he was in Colombia when a volcano erupted. His partner, Marianne, died in the eruption. Harry was very sad after that.

Now he was in Dante's Peak because his boss, Paul Dreyfus, sent him there. There were some signs that the volcano above the town might be waking up.

Harry met Rachel Wando, the mayor of Dante's Peak. She was a strong and kind woman with two children, Graham and Lauren. The town had just won the prize for the second-best small town in America, and everyone was very proud.

“Welcome to Dante's Peak,” Rachel said. “It's a wonderful place to live.”

Harry smiled, but he was worried. He looked up at the big mountain and thought, “I hope I'm wrong about this.”

Chapter 2 — Warning Signs

Strange things begin to happen near the mountain

The next day, Harry and Rachel went up the mountain together. Harry wanted to look at the hot springs near the top. When they got to one of the springs, they found something terrible. Two people were dead in the water. The water was too hot — it had boiled them.

“This is not normal,” Harry said. “The water is much hotter than it should be. Something is happening under the mountain.”

Harry checked the water in the river near the town. The water had too much acid in it. The fish in the river were dying. There were also small earthquakes — too small for most people to feel, but Harry's instruments could find them.

Harry told Rachel, “I think the volcano is becoming active again. We need to warn the people.”

Rachel was worried, but she also thought about the town. If people heard about the volcano, they would be afraid. Businesses might close. People might leave. She needed to think carefully about what to do.

Chapter 3 — The Investigation

Harry's team comes to study the volcano

Harry called his boss, Paul Dreyfus, and asked him to send a team. Paul came with several other scientists and a lot of equipment. They put instruments all around the mountain to measure earthquakes, temperature, and gases.

A robot called Spider Legs was sent into the crater at the top of the volcano. The robot could go where it was too dangerous for people. It sent back pictures and information about the gases inside the mountain.

The team worked for several days. Some of the data was worrying, but other data was normal. Paul was not sure that the volcano was really dangerous.

“Harry, we can't tell the whole town to leave because of a few hot springs and some small earthquakes,” Paul said. “We need more evidence.”

Harry was frustrated. He knew something was wrong. He could feel it.

Chapter 4 — Nobody Listens

Harry tries to convince people, but they don't believe him

There was a big town meeting. Many important business people came. They were excited about the “second-best small town” award. New people wanted to come and live in Dante's Peak. New businesses wanted to open there. It was a very good time for the town.

Harry stood up at the meeting and said, “I think we should put the town on alert. The volcano could be dangerous.”

The room went quiet. Then people started talking loudly. They were angry. The business people said, “You will destroy our town! Nobody will want to come here if you say there is a dangerous volcano!”

Paul Dreyfus told Harry to sit down. “We don't have enough evidence yet,” Paul said to the people. “Please don't worry. We are watching the mountain carefully.”

Harry was very angry with Paul. Rachel tried to help, but she was in a difficult position. She was the mayor — she had to think about the town's businesses AND the people's safety.

Chapter 5 — The Mountain Wakes

The volcano shows its power

Harry and Rachel were spending more time together. He liked her very much, and he liked her children too. But Harry never stopped thinking about the mountain.

One night, Harry and Rachel were at Rachel's house when the ground started to shake. It was a real earthquake this time — strong enough for everyone to feel. Glasses fell from the shelves. The children were frightened.

Then another earthquake came. And another. Harry ran to check his instruments. The readings were very bad. The mountain was definitely waking up.

Harry called Paul. “Paul, we have to evacuate the town. NOW.”

This time, Paul listened. He could hear the fear in Harry's voice, and the data was clear. “OK, Harry. Start the evacuation.”

Chapter 6 — Escape

The town tries to get away

The town sirens started. Rachel got on the radio and told everyone to leave the town. People ran to their cars. The roads were full of traffic.

But Rachel's children told her something terrible: “Grandma Ruth is still on the mountain!” Rachel's mother-in-law, Ruth, lived alone in a small house near the top of the mountain, by the lake. She refused to leave her home.

Rachel knew she had to get Ruth. Harry said, “I'll drive. Let's go.” They got into Harry's truck with the children and drove up the mountain as fast as they could. Everyone else was driving down the mountain, but they were going up.

When they arrived at Ruth's house, the old woman was sitting calmly in her kitchen. “I'm not leaving,” she said. “I've lived here for forty years.”

Then they heard a tremendous sound from the mountain. The ground shook violently. Smoke and ash started coming from the top.

“We have to go NOW!” Harry shouted. This time, even Ruth agreed.

Chapter 7 — The Eruption

The volcano erupts with terrible force

The volcano erupted. A huge cloud of ash and smoke went up into the sky. Hot rocks flew through the air. Lava started to flow down the mountain.

Harry drove the truck down the mountain road, but the road was blocked by fallen trees. They had to go a different way. Harry turned toward the lake. “We can cross the lake in Ruth's boat!”

They got to the lake and jumped into the small boat. But the acid from the volcano was in the lake water now. The water started to eat through the metal bottom of the boat. Water came in.

Ruth looked at her family. She loved them so much. Without saying a word, she stepped out of the boat into the acid water. She pushed the boat toward the shore. The acid burned her badly, but she saved her family.

They reached the shore, but Ruth was very badly hurt. She died in Rachel's arms. Everyone was crying.

Chapter 8 — Survival

Harry and the family fight to stay alive

Harry and the family ran to an old mine in the mountain. They went inside just as a huge flow of hot ash and rock came down the mountain. It was a pyroclastic flow — one of the most dangerous things a volcano can produce. It moves at hundreds of kilometers per hour and destroys everything.

Inside the mine, it was dark and dusty. They could hear the terrible sound of the eruption outside. The children were very scared. Rachel held them close.

Harry found his emergency beacon — a small device that sends a signal so rescue teams can find you. He turned it on and hoped that someone would come.

They waited in the dark mine for hours. Harry kept everyone calm. He told the children stories and made sure they had water. Rachel looked at Harry and knew he was a good man.

Chapter 9 — After the Disaster

Rescue and hope

The rescue teams found the signal from Harry's beacon. They came with big machines and started to dig through the ash and rock. It took many hours, but finally they reached the mine.

When the rescue workers opened the mine, Harry, Rachel, and the children were alive. They were dirty, tired, and sad about Ruth, but they were alive.

The town of Dante's Peak was buried under ash. Most of the buildings were destroyed. But because of the evacuation, most of the people were safe. Harry's warnings had saved many lives.

Harry looked at the mountain. It was quiet now. The eruption was over. He looked at Rachel and the children. He had lost so much before — Marianne, his old life. But now, maybe, he could start again.

Rachel took his hand. “Thank you, Harry,” she said. “Thank you for not giving up.”

Harry smiled. The mountain was quiet, and there was hope for a new beginning.
